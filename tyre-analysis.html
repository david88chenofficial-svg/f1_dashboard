Interpretation

Ax is the fitted longitudinal semi-axis and Ay is the fitted lateral semi-axis. Each lap uses the minimum-area, axis-aligned ellipse that contains at least 95% of its derived acceleration samples. Standing starts, pit laps, out laps, and statistically slow laps are listed separately and do not set the chart scale or stint trend.

This is an observed vehicle-usage envelope, not a direct measurement of tyre friction. Fuel load, aerodynamic load, traffic, track evolution, weather, and driver input are still mixed into the result. OpenF1 supplies event-relative labels; this lab joins a reviewed weekend allocation catalogue to show the corresponding C1–C6 construction when known.
